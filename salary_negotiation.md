# Salary Negotiation

## What is your expected salary?

I’m more interested in the right role and level. Based on market standards for Engineer-4 roles, I believe the compensation range is around 34–36 LPA. I’m open depending on the total package.

**If they ask again:**

Could you share the compensation range for this role?

Given my experience in leading Flutter architecture, performance optimization, and CI pipelines, I believe compensation closer to the top of the band would be appropriate.

I’m currently in discussions with another company offering around 32–34 LPA, but Comcast is my preferred choice. If we can get closer to 36 LPA, I’d be very happy to move forward.

## Negotiate the Breakdown

- increase joining bonus
- increase base pay
- retention bonus
- performance bonus
- medical insurance
- stock options
- meal passes
- other employee benefits

## Use the “Pause Technique”

When they give the offer:

Don't accept immediately.

**Say:**

“Thank you for the offer. I’m very interested in the role. Let me review the details and get back to you.”

Then respond next day asking for higher compensation.

## The Final Push Script

When they give a 30–32L offer, say:

“I’m really excited about the role and team. Considering my 9 years of experience and the responsibilities of Engineer-4, I was hoping we could get closer to 35 LPA. Is there any flexibility in the package?”

## if they are fine with joining bonus

**Ask HR clearly:**

“Is the joining bonus part of the CTC or paid separately on top of the compensation?”

Some companies give it outside CTC, which is better.

**Also ask:**

“Is there a clawback period if I leave within 1 year?”

Many companies require repayment if you leave early.
